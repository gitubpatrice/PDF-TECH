v1.13.5 — The app builds from its own sources

No functional change.

PDF Tech depended on an internal package by relative path, which only
resolves where the sibling repositories sit side by side. Anywhere else
— a plain clone, a CI run, a buildserver — resolution failed and the app
could not be built.

The package is now pulled from GitHub, pinned to an exact commit so the
result stays reproducible.
